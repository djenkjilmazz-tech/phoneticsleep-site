Frequently Asked Questions

Does sleep learning actually work?
Research on Targeted Memory Reactivation (TMR) shows that playing audio cues during sleep can reinforce memories formed while awake. PhoneticSleep uses this principle — consistent exposure to phoneme sounds during light sleep phases helps your brain familiarize with new sound patterns. Results vary by individual, sleep quality, and prior language exposure.
My purchase isn't showing up. What do I do?
Go to the app's Settings tab → tap "Restore Purchases". This will reconnect your App Store account and restore any previous purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase.
The app isn't playing sound while my screen is off.
Make sure Background App Refresh is enabled for PhoneticSleep in iOS Settings → General → Background App Refresh. Also check that the ringer switch on your iPhone is not set to silent, or use headphones. Low Power Mode can also interrupt background audio.
The alarm notification didn't fire at the right time.
Check that PhoneticSleep has notification permissions: iOS Settings → PhoneticSleep → Notifications → Allow Notifications must be on. Also ensure Do Not Disturb settings allow notifications from the app. If the issue persists, delete and re-set the alarm.
How do I cancel a subscription?
PhoneticSleep subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → PhoneticSleep → Cancel Subscription. You can also manage subscriptions at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How loud should I set the volume for sleep learning?
We recommend 20–30% volume — just barely audible while lying in bed. The sounds should be present but not disruptive. Too loud can interrupt sleep quality, which is counterproductive. Start low and adjust over a few nights.
Which theta frequency should I use?
6 Hz is the default and works well for most users. If you have trouble falling asleep, try 4–5 Hz (deeper, slower). If you're learning during light relaxation before sleep, 7–8 Hz is more appropriate. Experiment to find what works best for you.
Can I use PhoneticSleep with headphones?
Yes — and binaural beats work best with headphones. The binaural effect requires separate audio in each ear. Without headphones, both channels are mixed and the binaural effect is reduced. We recommend sleep-specific earbuds or flat-profile headphones for comfort.
How do I delete my data?
PhoneticSleep stores all data locally on your device. To delete all data, simply delete the app from your iPhone or iPad. There is no account or server data to request deletion of.
