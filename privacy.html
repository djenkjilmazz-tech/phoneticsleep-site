Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026

PhoneticSleep ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

PhoneticSleep does not directly collect any personally identifiable information. The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following data for analytics and ad serving purposes:

  • Device identifier (for ad frequency capping and fraud prevention)
  • General usage data (ad interactions)

This data is collected by Google and is not linked to your personal identity. No tracking is performed. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

3. Local Storage

The app stores your language preferences and session progress locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

4. Audio

The app plays audio for language learning purposes. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
